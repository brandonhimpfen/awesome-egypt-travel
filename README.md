## Official & Planning

Essential official tourism portals, electronic visa application systems, and antiquities authorities.

- [Experience Egypt (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.experienceegypt.eg/en) - Official promotional website of the Egyptian Tourism Authority featuring destination guides, cultural itineraries, and practical travel info.
- [Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/) - Official government portal for discovering Egypt's monuments, museums, archaeological sites, and sunken heritage.
- [Visa2Egypt (Official e-Visa Portal)](https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg/) - Official government electronic visa portal for secure tourist visa applications prior to arrival.

## Transportation

Navigating domestic aviation, Nile river cruise vessels, railways, and urban transit.

- [EgyptAir](https://www.egyptair.com/) - National flag carrier operating domestic flights connecting Cairo, Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada, and Sharm El Sheikh.
- [ENR (Egyptian National Railways)](https://new.enr.gov.eg/) - National railway network operating intercity passenger trains, including sleeper services between Cairo and Luxor/Aswan.
- [Grand Nile Cruise Guide](https://www.experienceegypt.eg/) - Official tourism resource for booking licensed multi-day river cruises between Luxor and Aswan.

## Destinations & Regions

### Cairo & Giza

- [Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)](https://gem.gov.eg/) - Official portal for visiting the world-class museum complex adjacent to the Giza pyramid plateau.
- [Cairo Governorate Tourism Portal](http://www.cairo.gov.eg/) - Official municipal resource for exploring Islamic Cairo, Khan el-Khalili bazaar, and Coptic Cairo landmarks.

### Luxor & Aswan (Nile Valley)

- [Luxor & Aswan Heritage Guide (Ministry of Antiquities)](https://egymonuments.gov.eg/) - Official visitor guides for the Valley of the Kings, Karnak Temple, Luxor Temple, and Abu Simbel.

### Alexandria & Mediterranean Coast

- [Alexandria Governorate Official Guide](https://alexandria.gov.eg/) - Municipal portal detailing the Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Qaitbay Citadel, and Mediterranean coastal attractions.

### Red Sea & Sinai Peninsula

- [Red Sea Tourism Board](https://www.experienceegypt.eg/) - Travel resources for resort hubs, scuba diving sites, and marine parks in Hurghada and Marsa Alam.
- [Sharm El Sheikh Official Portal](https://www.experienceegypt.eg/) - Visitor hub for Ras Mohamed National Park, diving excursions, and Sinai mountain trips.

## Safety & Practical Etiquette

Practical advice on health precautions, local clothing norms, currency handling, and emergency contacts.

- [Tourist Police Emergency Services](https://www.experienceegypt.eg/) - Dedicated tourist assistance division providing safety support and guidance across major heritage sites.
